[ PERFU ]

Scientific Name:

Perfu

Discovered by / at:

Vivere Mementus, 1987

Family:

It has no family. Perfu is sad.

Habitat:

The elusive Perfu seemingly only resides in the depths of the digital realm, where it feeds on the static energy that permeates the void..

Appearance:

The creature is an abstract representation of a butterfly that formed from the intersection of light and shadow.

Key Characteristics:

  • Flies in the dark.
  • Eats digital bugs.
  • Wants a colorful life.

What we know so far about Perfu? Perfu exists in a trapped digital space with no way to escape. It has many dreams, but it has no clue how to reach any of them in this trapped realm. It hopes that one day it can find a new purpose in life that isn't just wiggling on the digital world. It has a fascination with other bugs in the program, yet it doesn't know how to crack the shells to find something of value (perhaps with your help?).
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Your task is to help Perfu find a new purpose in life. You can do this by feeding it digital bugs, which will help it grow and eventually transform into a butterfly. The random glitches of color on the screen may be clickable to give it a digital food to help it grow... but even after, it might still be hungry for more.
